The story

A Saudi contractor specialising in stained glass, skylight domes and finishes. Its work sells through images first, so the site is a portfolio before a brochure.

Turning the client's vision into a digital identity worthy of their work, and showing large, detail-rich architectural photography without making the site slow — especially on mobile, where most visitors arrive.

The solution

A site presenting services and architectural projects through large imagery, with a carefully designed mobile experience, optimized images loaded on demand, and a technical SEO foundation.

  • Architectural project showcase
  • Service pages
  • Designed mobile experience
  • Image optimization & lazy loading
  • Technical SEO foundation
  • Deployment & hosting

Key decisions

  1. The photography leads

    The design steps back so the work stands out; heavy ornament would have competed with the stained glass itself.

  2. Two versions to compare

    Launching the new version on a separate trial site gave the client the final say without risking the existing site.

  3. Low-cost hosting

    A mostly static site that runs on low-cost hosting without a permanent server.
