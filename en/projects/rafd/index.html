The story

Students in virtual education face a particular challenge: the courses exist, but structured explanation and close follow-up aren't always there in the measure they need.

RAFD is an educational organization before it is code: it depends on great academics, student trust and protecting teachers' work.

The solution

A web platform with courses, library, quizzes and a registration simulator in one account, plus Windows and Android apps, with iOS in development.

  • Courses with recorded and live sessions
  • A library of files and summaries by course
  • Quizzes
  • Registration simulator
  • Academic calculators
  • Lecture and deadline calendar
  • Session playback
  • Offline download for permitted content
  • Playback speed control
  • Progress through content
  • Device & session access limits
  • Content protection measures

Key decisions

  1. Academics and students together

    Students know where peers struggle; academics know how understanding is built. Combining the two is the core of RAFD.

  2. Apps first

    Dedicated apps rather than a web page alone, for a better viewing experience and finer access control.

  3. Realistic protection

    Layers of protection that make misuse harder — without absolute promises that can't be kept.
