RAFD Financial System

An internal finance & operations system for the RAFD team

The story

As RAFD grew, its money branched out: subscriptions, payments, teacher payouts, expenses and partner profits — in three currencies.

Across scattered spreadsheets it's hard to see the whole picture: what came in, what went out, and who is owed.

The solution

A unified system that follows each student's financial cycle from registration to activation, computes payouts, revenue, expenses and partner shares, and produces financial reports.

  • Student counts
  • Registration & subscription states
  • Payments & activation
  • Teacher payouts
  • Platform revenue
  • Expenses
  • Partner profit-sharing
  • Financial reports
  • Multiple currencies

Key decisions

  1. A bespoke internal system

    The payout and profit logic is specific to the team; a bespoke system mirrors it exactly instead of working around a generic tool.

  2. Privacy first

    No student names, salaries or real profits appear anywhere on this site; any future screenshots will be redacted or use sample data.

Results & validation
The value is operational: one system that helps management understand results. No financial figures are published.
