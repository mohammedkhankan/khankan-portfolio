How I work

  • Understand the problem first

    Before any code: who uses this, and what does a mistake cost them?

  • Simple, without cutting corners

    The simplest solution that is correct — not the simplest that sometimes works.

  • Fast under real conditions

    On the networks and devices people actually use.

  • Protect data and permissions

    Everyone sees what's theirs and does only what they're allowed to.

  • Test the hard cases

    Double payments, concurrency, changing permissions — not just the happy path.

  • Technology by need

    Tools chosen because they fit, not because they trend.
