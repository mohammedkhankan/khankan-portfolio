About

An engineer who thinks like a business owner — and a founder who ships code.

  • Founder & CEO — OFOQ Technical Solutions & Digitalization
  • Founder & CEO — RAFD Educational Platform
  • Software engineer & SaaS product builder
  • Informatics Engineering — Syrian Virtual University
  • Saudi Arabia

  1. Foundation

    Engineering first

    He studied Informatics Engineering at the Syrian Virtual University, where his interest in systems took shape: how data is modelled, protected and kept fast.

  2. Observation

    From Saudi Arabia, looking at Syria

    In Saudi Arabia he used advanced digital services daily — and saw a real opportunity to bring such tools to small and mid-sized businesses in Syria.

  3. Founding

    RAFD

    He founded RAFD for supplementary learning; it now has 2,000+ subscribers. As much leadership as engineering: academics, contracts, apps and an internal finance system he built himself.

  4. Products

    OFOQ Technical Solutions & Digitalization

    Then he founded OFOQ Technical Solutions & Digitalization to build SaaS products: RUKN for stores, OFOQ for restaurants and MAAZEEM for events.

  5. Ownership

    From idea to production

    He leads each project from idea to launch, using AI tools and technical contributors where it helps — and owns the whole product and its decisions.

  6. Continuing

    Always learning

    Security and performance are a mindset from day one, not a final phase. Every product teaches something new.
